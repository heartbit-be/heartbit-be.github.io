# Heartbit

Vocabulary for describing Laurens's professional work and the businesses represented by Heartbit.

## Language

**Heartbit**:
The company umbrella for Laurens's freelance software work and a physical-product business with its own separate identity. Heartbit also serves as a contact point for custom 3D-printing work.

**Product business**:
The developing business selling physical items made with 3D printing, alone or combined with other materials, under an identity separate from Heartbit.

**AI-assisted software delivery**:
Laurens's use of coding agents to deliver software, which describes his current professional AI experience.

**AI-powered applications**:
Applications incorporating AI functionality, which Laurens plans to build. This is distinct from using coding agents to develop software.

**AI engineering**:
Laurens's offer to help teams adopt coding agents in development and to design and build AI-powered features and applications. His current professional experience is strongest in coding-agent use.

**Developer coaching**:
Laurens's ongoing support for developers within a team, through listening, follow-up, pair programming, and guidance. It describes his way of working, not a separate service package.

**Custom 3D-printing work**:
Enquiries for bespoke 3D-printing work that can come through Heartbit, alongside the separately branded product business. The product business may also accept such enquiries.
